# Perdido Peas
*A children's book by Ian MacCallum and Katie Rivas*

---

## Format

- **Trim size:** 8.5" x 8.5" square (standard picture book). Render at 2550 x 2550 px (300 DPI). Add 0.125" bleed later if we print.
- **Length:** Cover + dedication + 17 story pages.
- **Text placement:** Each page reserves a clear band (top or bottom, noted per page) for 2-4 lines of large, friendly text.

## Art style guide (applies to every page)

- Flat, warm, modern children's picture-book illustration. Thick rounded outlines, soft paper-grain texture, expressive faces, kid proportions (big heads, small bodies).
- **Palette:** emerald/turquoise Gulf water, sugar-white sand, sunshine yellow, coral, sky blue. School pages are muted gray-blue so the beach pages pop. Evening pages shift to gold, pink, and orange.
- **Recurring easter egg:** a small orange crab is hidden somewhere on every page (including the cover).
- **Eric's cap rule:** the cap is always backwards. Front views show the bill as a small tab behind the head on one side; in any side/profile shot the bill points fully behind him, never forward.
- Same character designs on every page (see character sheet). No style drift: same line weight, same palette, same proportions throughout.

## Critters (supporting cast; same outline and palette as the crab)

| Critter | Look | Appears |

| **Crab** | Small, orange, tiny sunglasses sometimes | Hidden on every page |

## Page 4
